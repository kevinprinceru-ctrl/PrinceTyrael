What Is TB-500?

TB-500 is a 43-amino acid peptide fragment derived from Thymosin Beta-4 — a protein naturally present in nearly every human and animal cell. It is one of the most studied peptides for tissue repair and recovery.

Promotes cell migration and tissue remodeling
Supports angiogenesis (formation of new blood vessels)
Modulates inflammation and reduces scar tissue
Enhances recovery from injury and surgery
Protects cells from damage and oxidative stress
"TB-500 accelerates healing and reduces inflammation by regulating the actin cytoskeleton, a key mechanism in cell movement and tissue repair." — Philpott, M. et al., Frontiers in Pharmacology, 2018
